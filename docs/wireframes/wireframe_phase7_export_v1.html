§06 · Size limits — soft warning + hard fail with EPUB-as-fallback
Soft warning at 3,000+ pages (DOCX)
This is a large export
Heads up: "Mars — Book 1" has 3,200 pages estimated. The export will take ~6-8 minutes and you'll see progress updates along the way. You can keep working while it runs.
Hard fail at 5,000+ pages (DOCX) — suggest EPUB
Document too large for DOCX
5,800 pages exceeds the DOCX export limit (5,000 pages).

For Kindle / KDP submission: EPUB is recommended and handles your size. Switch to EPUB now?

For editor handoff: you'll need to split this document into multiple parts (e.g. one DOCX per Act) and export each separately.

13 Pre-validation runs at Stage 1 of the pipeline. Soft warning at 3,000+ pages doesn't block; user confirms and proceeds. Hard fail at 5,000+ pages (DOCX) / 15,000+ pages (EPUB) blocks with a clear path forward.
14 EPUB-as-fallback suggestion is context-aware: prompts for switch when over DOCX limit but within EPUB limit. The copy explicitly differentiates publishing-path (EPUB fine) vs editor-handoff-path (must split). Decision Q9 (deep-dive) — author chooses; we advise.
