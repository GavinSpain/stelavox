Iteration 2 — changes from iter1
1
Wordmark scaled up ~20%. Cinzel 14→17, Cormorant 16→19, rule 80→100px, lozenge 7→9px with stronger glow. Header height also bumped 48→52px to give the wordmark visual room. Compared to other SaaS apps: Linear ~17px, Notion ~16px, Figma ~18px, Vercel ~16px. We're now in the middle of that range.
2
Sidebar hairline louder. Gradient opacity bumped from 35% → 55% at peak. Visible as a defined brand element now rather than "is that something or just my screen?"
3
Sandbox concept dropped. Test-fixture projects (exec-pw-*) are deleted from the DB outright as a one-time cleanup. The disclosure block is gone. Only Shadow Protocol remains.
4
Continue writing →. NEW per-card affordance at the bottom of each doc card, verdigris-tinted text link. The single most-likely action from dashboard is "open the document I was last in and keep going" — making it explicit (not just "click anywhere on the card") gives the eye a target.
5
"New document" flow corrected. The card-slot placeholder is gone from the dashboard. Documents live inside projects, so the dashboard primary CTA is "+ New project" (verdigris, use #7 affirmative-action); per-project rows carry a small "+ Document" button that knows its parent project. Cmd+K palette will also support "new document" with a project picker — but the visual-affordance gap on dashboard is closed.
6
Per-project "+ Document" affordance. Tucked alongside the project's arrow, neutral chrome, verdigris-on-hover. Doesn't fight for attention with "+ New project" (the primary CTA) but is reachable in one click without entering the project.

03 — Writer's eye · my honest reaction
A writer opens Stelavox at 9:14am with their first coffee

I'm imagining I sat down at my desk yesterday and wrote half a chapter of Shadow Protocol. This morning I want to keep going. I open the laptop, navigate to the bookmark, the login is muscle memory, and now I'm here. What do I want, what do I see?

What works

The wordmark at the top-left finally says "this is a craft tool" instead of "this is a CRUD app." The sidebar hairline is small but the eye registers it — peripheral confirmation that I'm in a designed space. The Recent Documents card carries enough information that I don't need to click into the project just to remember where I am: it tells me I was at 38% completion, that the Director is currently working on something, and that "last edit 12 min ago" matches my mental clock. "Continue writing →" gives my eye a single target — I press that and I'm in the prose, no detours.

What still feels off, after iter2
R-1
Only one document, but the doc grid takes half the page. When there's one doc and one project, the dashboard reads "2/3 of this screen is empty space and dashed placeholders". The dashed second-slot card is a half-measure — it works as a Fitts target but it doesn't carry information. Suggestion: when the doc count is ≤1, collapse to a single full-width doc card with richer information (recent activity preview, the last sentence I wrote, today's word count target vs actual), and only switch to the grid when there are ≥2 documents.
R-2
No sense of how much I wrote yesterday / this week. Writers track output. There's no streak indicator, no "1,247 words yesterday", no "you're on day 4 of 7". This is the kind of thing that makes someone come back. Could be a slim band above Recent Documents — "Today: 0 words · Yesterday: 1,247 · 7-day total: 8,432 · 12-day streak" — Cormorant Garamond italic for the numbers, very subtle, but always there.
R-3
The greeting is the same forever. "Good afternoon, Author." after the first day of use becomes pure chrome. Iterating ideas: greeting cycles through a small set of quiet phrases ("Where were we, Author?", "Welcome back.", "Day 12.") — or the greeting can be replaced after first-visit-of-the-day by the most recent paragraph the author wrote, in Lora 18px italic, indented, like a journal returning to itself. That's a touch I think makes this a "writer's tool" rather than "doc tool."
R-4
Director status is in the doc card, but the Director itself is invisible from dashboard. If I want to talk to the Director without first opening the document, I can't. Should the Director status pip be clickable — opens a small popover with the most recent message + "Reply" link? Or is that overload? Honest answer: probably overload for V1, but worth flagging because it'll come up.
R-5
"All documents" and "All projects" actions feel redundant in a 1-doc 1-project world. Once you have 8 projects and 30 documents you'll want them. At V1 launch they're chrome noise. Consider hiding these section-header actions when the underlying count is small enough that everything's already on screen.
R-6
The sidebar duplicates the Projects list in the main area. "Shadow Protocol" appears in the sidebar and in the project row beneath Recent Documents. With more projects this gets noisy. Suggestion: sidebar shows only pinned/recent projects (max ~5), with "All projects" expanding the rest in-place or routing to a separate list view. We don't need to solve this for V1's 1-project state, but the pattern should be set before it becomes ugly at 10+.
R-7
The dashboard doesn't say what's NEXT. Card shows "Director: synthesising prose for Act 2 hook (3 beats)" — but I don't see "3 beats waiting for your accept" or "nothing for you right now". The first thing a writer wants to know on returning is "do I have to do anything, or can I just write?". A single line answering that — maybe under the greeting, in a quieter colour — would do a lot of work.
R-8
Pulsing status pip on agent-running is correct, but only on the dashboard. If I'm scanning peripherally to see "are we still working?", the pulse is exactly right. But once I'm in Edit Mode, watching it pulse forever in the corner becomes a distraction. The animation should pause when the user is actively typing (autosave keystroke recency) and resume when they idle. Out of scope for this dashboard wireframe but flagging for Edit Mode v2.
Honest gut feel

The page is meaningfully better than iter1. The wordmark + hairline give it "this is Stelavox" the instant you see it. The doc card gives you actionable state at a glance. The greeting plus the "Continue writing →" link gives me a clear next step. But I think it's still 40% chrome and 60% empty for the 1-doc state. The single biggest improvement we could make is R-1: collapse the doc grid to a single richer card when there's one doc — fill the page with what matters. R-2 and R-3 are the personality touches that would make a writer come back daily.

04 — Decisions (iteration 2 deltas)
Iteration 2 changes
Wordmark size
Cinzel 14 → 17, Cormorant 16 → 19, rule 80 → 100px, lozenge 7 → 9px (with stronger glow). Header height 48 → 52px. Comparable to Linear/Notion/Figma/Vercel wordmark scale.
Hairline opacity
35% → 55% gradient peak. Visible as a designed element now.
Sandbox section
Removed entirely. Cleanup task: delete exec-pw-* projects (and any other test-fixture projects) from the DB. Preserve Shadow Protocol. Tracked as part of 8.01 close-out.
"New document" flow
Card-slot placeholder removed from dashboard. Primary CTA: "+ New project" (verdigris). Per-project "+ Document" affordance on each project row (neutral chrome, verdigris-on-hover). Cmd+K palette will support "new document" with project picker. This is the corrected mental model: dashboard → projects → documents.
Continue writing →
NEW per-doc-card affordance. Verdigris-tinted text link. Single-target eye-anchor for the most likely action from dashboard.
Writer's-eye recommendations · open
R-1 collapsed solo-doc card
Single full-width card when doc count ≤1, with richer information panel. Strongly recommend. The biggest single win for the empty-feel problem.
R-2 writing stats band
Thin "Today / yesterday / streak / target" line. Highly characterful. Recommend.
R-3 dynamic greeting
Cycling greeting OR last-paragraph echo. Personality touch. Like the last-paragraph idea — has weight. Recommend for iter3.
R-4 Director popover from card
Status pip becomes clickable popover. Defer — likely overload for V1.
R-5 hide "All …" at low counts
Section-header actions visible only when there's more than fits the visible region. Cheap recommendation.
R-6 sidebar dedup
Cap sidebar projects at ~5, route the rest behind "All projects." Pattern-set for scale. Flag for iter3 or Edit Mode v2.
R-7 "what's next for me" line
Quiet single sentence under greeting answering "do I have to do anything?". Recommend.
R-8 pulse pause on type
Out of scope for dashboard. Flag for Edit Mode v2.
