03 — Writer's eye · iter3
Re-reading the tree at 3pm with a real document in mind

Two things have to be true at once. The tree has to disappear when I'm writing — I want to find a beat, click it, and start typing without the interface narrating itself. The tree has to wake up when I'm planning — when I'm scanning the structure for "what needs doing next," it needs to communicate state clearly without me having to lean in. Iter3 lands closer to that balance than iter1 did.

What works in iter3

The bracketed labels mean I never have to count indents to know what a row is. [Bt 3] Recognition tells me everything in a glance — it's Beat 3, named Recognition, on the third row of its parent. The position-number-from-data is the win. Before, the tree just said "Recognition" and I had to know it was the second beat under The Iron Ghost; now the row tells me.

The standardised word-count column is the right unit-of-progress at every layer. Looking at the Act 1 row I see 7,824 / 12,500 and a 62%-full bar — I know exactly how much more I have to write at this level. Looking at the Book row tells me 38% of the whole novel. Looking at a beat tells me 320/300 in green (over target but completed) or 410/300 in amber (over target, needs review). The eye finds problems by colour — amber jumps out among green.

The hover action strip is the right pattern. I never want to open the detail pane just to fire a Refine command — that's three clicks to do one thing. Now it's: hover, click Refine. The strip vanishes when I move my mouse away — quiet at rest, present on demand.

What I'd push or revise
R-1
The word-count column is taking ~145px of horizontal space on every row. That's fine on a 1280px screen but borderline on iPad and tight if the detail pane is also visible. Counter-proposal: word-count column collapses to just the bar at narrow widths, with the ratio shown on hover or in the detail pane. Or: word-count column appears only at parent rows (Book / Act / Chapter), with per-beat counts shown in the detail pane. The data is always there; the question is how much real-estate it deserves.
R-2
The "3 running" / "3 new" / "Brief Active" lifecycle pills on parent rows are useful but the count can mislead. "Act 1: 3 running" — three beats? Three scenes? Three of anything? The eye guesses. Consider adding the unit: "3 beats running", "3 new beats". Small text bump, removes ambiguity.
R-3
The chevron on parent rows reads as decorative, not interactive. Right now it's ▸ (collapsed) / ▾ (expanded) at opacity:0.85. To make it feel like an affordance, increase opacity on parent rows OR make the entire chev + label + name area the click target for expand-collapse (separate from row-select). Current behaviour ambiguous — does clicking a parent row expand it or just select it?
R-4
Hover action strip on parent rows feels heavy. Five actions appearing every time I hover a chapter or act might be noisier than the value justifies. Consider: only show on leaf rows (beats), where the actions are most natural. Parent rows could show a simpler "Open" button (or nothing — clicking expands/selects and that's enough). Test in real use.
R-5
The Series-of-Novels example renders fine, but I want to see what 200 nodes looks like. Theoretical scaling works in a 15-row mock; real authors will have trees of 200+. Worth a stress-test wireframe at high depth + breadth. iter4 candidate: "Edit Mode · Heavy Tree" showing a 250-node tree with realistic density.
R-6
The detail pane title "[Act 1] The Salvage" works. The same bracketed-label pattern from the tree shows up in the detail pane header, so the eye knows it's looking at the same row it just clicked. Visual continuity. Keep this pattern in every node-detail context (Director references, comments, jobs, history).
R-7
The "Director working" status pill at bottom-right plus the "Director" tab dot at top means there are two indicators of the same thing. They can drift (one updates, the other doesn't). Single source of truth or live-bind both. Engineering note, not a design one.
Honest gut feel

This is the first iteration where I'd actually want to open Stelavox and start writing. The tree is no longer "what does this row mean?" — it's "where am I, what's done, what's running, what needs me." The visual language scales (the Series-of-Novels example proves it). The two unsolved items are the iPad layout (T-8) and the non-leaf detail pane shape (T-5) — both worth their own wireframes rather than being squeezed into this one. For me, iter3 is good enough to move on to those two and to Director Mode.

04 — Decisions iter3
Locked from prior iterations
Label format
Bracketed monospace ([Book 1], [Act 1], [Ch 1], [Sc 1], [Bt 1]). JetBrains Mono / ui-monospace. Per-layer colour as secondary cue only.
Abbreviation form
Book / Act / Ch / Sc / Bt — short labels full word, longer labels abbreviated. Series gets [Series N].
Position source
Numbers come from nodes.order. No UI-side counting. Drift impossible.
Director @-reference
Same vocabulary — @series1book1act1ch2sc3bt1 resolves via the project's layer_stack. Functional work is Phase 14 / 8.03; visual language locked now.
No universal Series wrapper
Project = generic workspace. Series appears as a node_type in some layer_stacks (Series-of-Novels, TV Series, Anthology). Not a special row above the tree.
New in iter3
Layer column header
Demoted to lightweight monospace legend ([Book] [Act] [Ch] [Sc] [Bt]) — orientation only, not load-bearing. The row-level bracketed labels do the layer-identification work.
Word-count column (T-2)
Standardised at every row with a target: mini bar + actual/target ratio, right-aligned. Aggregates upward. Amber when over target. Green when complete. Muted under.
Indent guides (T-4)
Vertical guides at opacity 0.22-0.32 with subtle per-layer tint. Visible without dominating.
Hover action strip (T-6)
Compact action bar appears on row hover. Actions vary by node type: beats get Refine + Synthesise + Comment + Lock; parents get + Expand + Refine + Comment + Lock. Primary action in verdigris, destructive in amber.
Detail pane label
Title row carries the same bracketed label as the tree row — visual continuity. Layer-tinted.
Stack badge
Small Novel / Series of Novels / etc. badge near the document title — communicates the layer_stack at a glance. Verdigris-bordered.
