04 — Sidebar empty-state research (F-6)

// what other writing/productivity apps do when the sidebar has nothing to show.

AppPatternWhy it works (or doesn't)
Notion Templates browser + Getting Started checklist Templates are concrete starting points; the checklist progresses as you use the app. Strong for fresh users.
Linear "My Issues" empty state with sample issue suggestion Doesn't pretend the sidebar is full — shows the empty list with an action ("Create your first issue"). Honest.
Figma Recents + Community templates + "New design file" CTA Templates dominate. Sidebar is short and clean; the canvas has the rich onboarding.
Bear Tags list (empty: "Add your first tag") Minimal; respects that some apps are quiet by design. Author-friendly.
Scrivener Project templates upfront in a separate launcher window Templates are the entire entry experience. Heavyweight for the modern web but the principle is right: pick a shape, then write.
Ulysses Library structure pre-built with sample sheets You see the shape immediately. Reduces the "blank page" intimidation. Strong for writers.

06 — Writer's eye review

What worked. The single "Get started" CTA backed by "Try the sample novel" gives an out for the cautious user without forcing a wizard on the eager one. The "Yours" tile with the encryption line is the most concrete brand-trust signal we can offer truthfully — and it sits in the third tile so it lands as the closing note, not the lead. The Quick Start checklist gives the sidebar something to do without forcing chrome on returning users (it disappears once setup is complete).

What I'd watch in real use.

  • D-1 — "Encrypted at rest" wording. Accurate (Postgres + Vault) without overclaiming end-to-end. Alternative phrasings: "PRIVATE TO YOUR ACCOUNT" alone, or "ENCRYPTED · YOUR ACCOUNT ONLY". Recommend keeping the two-line form as drawn.
  • D-2 — Quick Start condensed state. After all five items complete, drop to a single line ("Setup complete ✓") for one session then remove. Or keep as a permanent reference card? Recommend remove — it's onboarding scaffolding, not a permanent fixture.
  • D-3 — Sample novel disclaimer. Should the sample be tagged in the project list ("SAMPLE" label) so the author doesn't mistake it for their own work? Recommend yes — small monospace "SAMPLE" badge in the project card and tree header.
  • D-4 — Tagline. "A hierarchical writing workspace where structure stays in your hands and AI helps where you ask it to." Long. Could shorten to: "Hierarchical writing. AI that proposes, never decides." Recommend the longer form — the brand value of "structure stays in your hands" is the differentiator.
  • D-5 — Three-tile vs four. Considered adding a fourth tile for "Export anywhere" but it duplicates the "Yours" tile message. Three tiles read better.
